# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Break Anything to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of
experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Assume good intent; critique ideas, not people.
- Keep security research responsible and lawful — on targets you own or are authorized to test.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sharing others' private information without consent.
- Using the project or its community to coordinate attacks on systems you do not control.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code, and
may ban repeat or severe offenders. Report concerns to the maintainers privately (see
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for contact).

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
